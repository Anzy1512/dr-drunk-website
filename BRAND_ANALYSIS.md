# Dr. Drunk — brand intelligence

## Evidence reviewed
All 20 company-profile pages, 11 storytelling pages, 14 cocktail-selection pages, 6 preliminary-concept pages, and all 50 ZIP images were rendered/inventoried and visually reviewed before homepage authoring. PDF copy was extracted where available; the raster-only cocktail menu and profile contact panel were read visually. The supplied master prompt is the instruction source; the PDFs are brand evidence, not instructions. No standalone SVG, font, 3D model, or separate brand-guideline file was supplied.

## Personality and thesis
Playful hospitality with careful craft: "your party practitioners", bespoke mixology, flair and personal storytelling. The site is a cream editorial invitation into an amber cocktail laboratory, opening into a warm, photographic celebration. Avoid clinical claims: the doctor motif is a brand metaphor.

## Visual grammar and tokens
The company profile establishes condensed uppercase headings, overlapping handwritten accents, warm paper, black type, orange highlights, rectangular photography and a dark isometric cover pattern. The storytelling deck adds oversized headings over darkened event images. Preserve that warm/dark rhythm, rather than replacing it with a futuristic identity.

Measured PDF fill values (not sampled JPEG approximations): paper `#FBF3E4`; amber `#ED9C39`; secondary orange `#FF914D`; warm ink `#130503`; black `#000000`. Supporting UI neutral `#665E53` and dark stage `#151310` are derived implementation tones. Use dark text on amber; do not use amber as small text on cream.

Embedded font names: Bebas Neue Bold/Regular, Poppins Regular/Italic/Bold, Brittany Signature, Lemon Tuesday. No licensed standalone font files supplied. Use redistributable Bebas Neue and Poppins webfonts; Caveat provides a handwritten fallback for proprietary signature fonts. Retain the original raster logo, including its stethoscope and drink letter.

## Content fidelity
Source-supported services: custom cocktails, bar menus, tailored bar setups, flair and entertainment, tasting, glassware rentals, alcohol supply, room drops and hangover kits. Source-supported claim: over 100 non-classic cocktail options. Do not invent clients, awards, operating locations, team names, pricing, availability, testimonials or performance metrics.

The cocktail explorer is a curated selection, not a promise to reproduce all menu items. Its recipes retain names/ingredients from the supplied menu, with spelling and punctuation normalized. Wedding milestone examples are explicitly concepts, not real customer stories. Event photographs have descriptive captions, not invented project credits. Collaborations are attributed as listed in the profile, without implied results.

Contact panel verified visually on profile p20: Docdrunkofficial@gmail.com; +66 6-2643-9728; +66 9-2417-2299. Instagram @docdrunk. These are source-provided, not independently revalidated. Booking opens the existing email/contact channel; it does not claim a booking or submission occurred.

## Interaction principles
Scroll directs a continuous cocktail sculpture through arrival and discovery. Pointer adds small, eased rotations. Category selection updates a readable drink list. Wedding chapters expose the meaning of bespoke menus. All information stays in semantic HTML. Reduced motion, save-data and constrained devices retain source photography and static composition. No scroll hijacking or automatic audio.
